# AgentSLA Evidence — agentsla-cyber-003

This evidence document maps each incident in `report.json` to at least two credible public sources.

The report deliberately distinguishes confirmed facts from attacker claims and avoids treating unverified record counts or attribution claims as established facts.

## 1. Boston Scientific Corporation

Announcement date: 2026-08-26

Primary / authoritative source:
https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/885725/000088572526000056/bsx-20260826.htm

Additional authoritative source:
https://news.bostonscientific.com/update-on-recent-cybersecurity-incident

Evidence supported:
- Boston Scientific identified a cybersecurity incident on August 25, 2026.
- Certain IT systems were affected.
- The incident caused a network outage and operational disruption.
- Systems supporting order processing and shipping were affected.
- The company activated incident response and engaged external cybersecurity experts.

## 2. U.S. Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives (ATF)

Public disclosure window: 2026-08-26 onward

Authoritative source:
https://www.atf.gov/news/press-releases/atf-update-cybersecurity-incident

Independent reporting:
https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/us-federal-agency-confirms-data-breach-wake-claims-by-ransomware-group-2026-08-27/

Evidence supported:
- ATF confirmed a cybersecurity incident involving a standalone system.
- The system contained information related to investigative matters.
- The affected standalone system was separate from ATF's main operational systems.
- ATF said the incident did not affect its enterprise network or eForms systems.
- ATF and the Department of Justice investigated the incident.

## 4. McKesson Corporation

Announcement date: 2026-08-28

Authoritative source:
https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/927653/000092765326000247/mck-20260825.htm

Independent cybersecurity reporting:
https://therecord.media/mckesson-cyberattack-ransomware-pharma

Evidence supported:
- McKesson discovered a cybersecurity incident on August 25, 2026.
- The company publicly disclosed the incident on August 28.
- Subsequent information confirmed unauthorized access to third-party applications and data exfiltration.
- A subset of customers in Oncology & Multispecialty and Medical-Surgical business units was associated with the exfiltrated data.
- Public reporting described intermittent service degradation.
- Unverified attacker claims about total stolen-record counts are intentionally excluded as confirmed facts.

## 5. Thomson Reuters / C-Track

Announcement date: 2026-09-02 / 2026-09-03 public disclosure window

Independent reporting:
https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/thomson-reuters-detects-cybersecurity-incident-says-unauthorized-party-accessed-2026-09-03/

Authoritative affected-court statement:
https://www.ontariocourts.ca/en/public-statement-cybersecurity.htm

Evidence supported:
- Thomson Reuters detected unauthorized activity involving C-Track on June 30, 2026.
- Investigation determined that certain C-Track files had been obtained by an unauthorized party earlier in the year.
- C-Track is used by multiple court systems.
- Ontario's three Chief Justices publicly stated that some Ontario court information was involved.
- Thomson Reuters undertook containment, external investigation, law-enforcement notification and customer notification.

## Evidence-policy check

- Exactly five incidents are mapped.
- Every incident has at least two credible public sources.
- Government, regulator, company or court sources are used wherever available.
- Independent reporting is used as corroboration.
- Material claims in `report.json` are traceable to the sources above.
