Two-sided evidence

The requester gets a protected challenge window.

The provider commits work first; the requester may then commit one SHA-256-bound counter-evidence artifact before review.

Named authorities

Every SLA commits two exact sources.

Both authority URLs and their expected SHA-256 digests are immutable terms that validators authenticate before judgment.

Protocol lifecycle

Simple escrow logic, intelligent judgment only where needed.

Deterministic rules control identity, deadlines, hashes, the challenge period and payout. GenLayer consensus is used only for the categorical fulfillment decision.

01

Create + fund

Requester escrows GEN and commits terms plus two authority sources.

02

Accept terms

The named provider explicitly accepts the exact terms hash.

03

Submit work

Provider work and evidence are committed with SHA-256 digests.

04

Challenge

Requester may commit counter-evidence before the fixed deadline.

05

Consensus review

Validators authenticate every artifact and agree on a categorical verdict.

06

Settle

SATISFIED pays the provider; UNSATISFIED refunds the requester.
